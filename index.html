Gmail Invite Details: Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

This extension reads the calendar invite inside an email you open in Gmail and shows its meeting details on the page. Everything happens in your browser. It collects nothing, stores nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

The policy in full

  1. What the extension accesses. When you open an email in Gmail that contains a calendar invite, the extension reads that email's raw content (the same text Gmail shows under "Show original") or its .ics attachment. It looks only at emails you open, and only for the calendar invite.
  2. What it does with it. It pulls out the meeting title, join link, meeting ID, passcode and dial-in details and shows them in a box on the Gmail page. Nothing is changed in your mailbox.
  3. What it stores. Nothing. No email content, invite details or settings are saved to disk, to browser storage or anywhere else.
  4. What it sends. Nothing leaves your browser. The only network requests are to Gmail's own addresses (mail.google.com and mail-attachment.googleusercontent.com), using your existing signed-in session, to fetch the email you are already viewing.
  5. Sharing and selling. No data is sold, shared, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose other than showing you the meeting details.
  6. Google API data. Use of information received from Gmail follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  7. Copy buttons. When you click Copy, the chosen value is put on your clipboard. The extension never reads your clipboard.
  8. Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
